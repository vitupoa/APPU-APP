# Super Admin Ad Management System

## Overview
Complete admin control panel for managing all aspects of the advertising platform including pricing, placement, platform revenue split, campaign approval, and analytics.

---

## Admin Dashboard Features

### 1. Platform Revenue Split Control

**Default**: 15% platform, 85% creator (industry-leading)

**Admin Controls**:
- Adjust platform cut percentage (1-50%)
- Real-time calculation preview
- Example: KES 10,000 sponsored post
  - Platform (15%): KES 1,500
  - Creator (85%): KES 8,500

**Why 15% is Optimal**:
- **Instagram**: Takes 45% (55% to creator)
- **YouTube**: Takes 45% (55% to creator)
- **TikTok**: Takes 50% (50% to creator)
- **Krieta**: Takes 15% (85% to creator) - **INDUSTRY-LEADING**

**Benefits**:
- Attracts top creators from other platforms
- Creators earn 30-35% more than competitors
- Viral word-of-mouth marketing
- Creator loyalty and platform lock-in

---

### 2. Ad Pricing Management

#### Native Feed Ads
- **Current Range**: KES 40-150 CPM
- **Admin Controls**: Set min/max CPM
- **Recommendation**: Stay 20-40% below Facebook (KES 50-200)

#### Banner Ads
- **Current Range**: KES 30-100 CPM
- **Admin Controls**: Set min/max CPM or daily flat rate
- **Recommendation**: Cheapest option for brand awareness

#### Story-Style Ads
- **Current Range**: KES 80-200 CPM
- **Admin Controls**: Set min/max CPM
- **Recommendation**: Competitive with Instagram Stories

#### Sponsored Posts
- **Current Range**: KES 2,000-50,000 per post
- **Admin Controls**: Set min/max pricing tiers
- **Tiers**:
  - Micro (1k-10k): KES 2,000-5,000
  - Mid (10k-50k): KES 5,000-15,000
  - Macro (50k-200k): KES 15,000-50,000
  - Mega (200k+): KES 50,000+

---

### 3. Ad Placement Frequency Control

**Current Settings**:
- Native Ads: Every 6 posts
- Banner Ads: Every 15 posts
- Story Ads: Every 20 posts
- Max Ad Load: 20% of feed

**Admin Controls**:
- Adjust frequency for each ad type (1-50 posts)
- Set max ad load percentage (5-30%)
- Toggle ads on/off globally
- A/B test different frequencies

**Best Practices**:
- Keep ad load under 20% (user experience)
- Native ads most frequent (blend with content)
- Banner ads less frequent (more intrusive)
- Story ads least frequent (full-screen)

---

### 4. Campaign Management

#### Campaign Statuses
1. **Draft**: Advertiser creating campaign
2. **Pending Approval**: Submitted for admin review
3. **Approved**: Admin approved, ready to run
4. **Active**: Currently running
5. **Paused**: Temporarily stopped
6. **Completed**: Budget exhausted or end date reached
7. **Rejected**: Admin rejected (policy violation)

#### Admin Actions
- **Approve Campaign**: Review creative, targeting, budget
- **Reject Campaign**: Provide reason (policy violation, inappropriate content)
- **Pause Campaign**: Stop serving ads temporarily
- **Delete Campaign**: Permanently remove
- **Edit Campaign**: Modify budget, targeting, creative

#### Approval Checklist
✅ Creative meets quality standards (min 800x800px)  
✅ No prohibited content (alcohol, gambling, adult)  
✅ Clear CTA and landing page  
✅ Targeting is reasonable (not discriminatory)  
✅ Budget is sufficient (min KES 500)  
✅ Advertiser account verified

---

### 5. Sponsored Posts Management

#### Workflow
1. **Brand Creates Offer**: KES 5,000 for 1 post
2. **Creator Accepts**: Agreement formed
3. **Creator Posts**: With #ad or #sponsored
4. **Admin Verifies**: Post meets requirements
5. **Payment Released**: 
   - Creator: KES 4,250 (85%)
   - Platform: KES 750 (15%)

#### Admin Controls
- View all sponsored post requests
- Verify post meets brand requirements
- Approve/reject payment release
- Handle disputes between brand and creator
- Adjust platform cut for specific deals

#### Verification Checklist
✅ Post includes #ad or #sponsored hashtag  
✅ Content matches brand brief  
✅ No misleading claims  
✅ Proper disclosure (FTC compliance)  
✅ Creator hasn't deleted post prematurely

### 6. Analytics Dashboard

#### Platform-Wide Metrics
- **Total Revenue**: KES earned from all ad types
- **Active Campaigns**: Currently running campaigns
- **Total Impressions**: Ads shown to users
- **Total Clicks**: User interactions with ads
- **Average CTR**: Click-through rate across all ads
- **Fill Rate**: % of ad slots filled
- **Creator Earnings**: Total paid to creators

#### Campaign-Specific Metrics
- **Impressions**: Times ad was shown
- **Clicks**: Times ad was clicked
- **CTR**: Click-through rate (clicks/impressions)
- **CPM**: Cost per 1000 impressions
- **CPC**: Cost per click
- **Spend**: Total budget spent
- **Remaining Budget**: Budget left
- **Conversions**: Actions taken (if tracked)

#### Revenue Breakdown
- Native Feed Ads: KES X/month
- Banner Ads: KES X/month
- Story Ads: KES X/month
- Sponsored Posts: KES X/month
- **Total**: KES X/month

### 7. Ad Quality Control

#### Automated Checks
- Image resolution (min 800x800px)
- File size (max 5MB)
- Text length (max 125 char headline, 200 char description)
- Prohibited keywords detection
- NSFW content detection (AI)

#### Manual Review
- Creative quality assessment
- Brand safety check
- Landing page verification
- Targeting appropriateness
- Budget reasonableness

#### Rejection Reasons
- ❌ Low-quality creative
- ❌ Prohibited content (alcohol, gambling, adult)
- ❌ Misleading claims
- ❌ Broken landing page
- ❌ Discriminatory targeting
- ❌ Insufficient budget

---

### 8. Fraud Prevention

#### Detection Methods
- **Device Fingerprinting**: Track unique devices
- **IP Monitoring**: Detect bot farms
- **Click Pattern Analysis**: Identify suspicious behavior
- **Conversion Tracking**: Verify real actions
- **Advertiser Verification**: KYC checks

#### Admin Actions
- Flag suspicious campaigns
- Refund fraudulent charges
- Ban fraudulent advertisers
- Blacklist IP addresses
- Report to authorities (if criminal)

---

### 9. Creator Payout Management

#### Payout Schedule
- **Frequency**: Weekly (every Monday)
- **Minimum**: KES 500
- **Method**: M-Pesa, Airtel Money, Bank Transfer
- **Processing Time**: 1-3 business days

#### Admin Controls
- View pending payouts
- Approve/reject payout requests
- Handle payout disputes
- Adjust minimum payout threshold
- Change payout frequency

#### Payout Verification
✅ Creator has valid M-Pesa number  
✅ Earnings exceed minimum threshold  
✅ No pending disputes  
✅ Account in good standing  
✅ Tax information provided (if required)

### 10. Advertiser Management

#### Advertiser Tiers
1. **Self-Serve**: Small businesses (KES 500-10,000/month)
2. **Managed**: Medium businesses (KES 10,000-100,000/month)
3. **Enterprise**: Large businesses (KES 100,000+/month)

#### Admin Controls
- View all advertisers
- Verify business information
- Approve/reject advertiser accounts
- Set spending limits
- Provide white-glove support (enterprise)

#### Verification Requirements
- Business name and registration
- Contact information
- M-Pesa/payment details
- Business category
- Website/social media

## Summary

### What Was Built
1. **AdConfig Model**: Platform cut, pricing, frequency settings
2. **AdCampaign Model**: Campaign management with approval workflow
3. **SponsoredPostRequest Model**: Creator collaboration tracking
4. **AdminAdManagementScreen**: Full UI with 4 tabs (Config, Campaigns, Sponsored Posts, Analytics)
5. **AdManagementViewModel**: All control functions
6. **AdManagementRepository**: Interface for backend integration

### Key Features
- **Platform Cut Control**: Adjust 15% default (industry-leading 85% to creators)
- **Pricing Management**: Set CPM ranges for all ad types
- **Frequency Control**: Adjust ad placement (every N posts)
- **Campaign Approval**: Review and approve/reject campaigns
- **Sponsored Post Verification**: Verify posts meet requirements
- **Analytics Dashboard**: Platform-wide and campaign-specific metrics
- **Fraud Prevention**: Device fingerprinting, IP monitoring, pattern analysis

### Why 15% Platform Cut Works
- **Attracts Creators**: 85% is 30% more than Instagram/YouTube (55%)
- **Competitive Advantage**: Industry-leading split
- **Creator Loyalty**: Once earning, creators stay and promote platform
- **Viral Growth**: Word-of-mouth from happy creators
- **Sustainable**: 15% still profitable at scale (KES 75k/month from sponsored posts alone)

### Revenue Impact (Year 3)
- **Old Model (40% cut)**: KES 200k/month from sponsored posts
- **New Model (15% cut)**: KES 75k/month from sponsored posts
- **Difference**: -KES 125k/month (-62.5%)
- **BUT**: More creators join → more content → more users → more ad impressions → higher overall revenue
- **Net Effect**: Short-term revenue loss, long-term revenue gain through network effects

---

**BOTTOM LINE**: Super admin has complete control over ad pricing, placement, platform cut, and campaign approval. The 15% platform cut (85% to creators) is industry-leading and will attract top creators from Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok, driving viral growth and long-term revenue.
